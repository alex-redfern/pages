      House rules

      • Never acids and retinoid the same night.Leave a clear night between them. Saturday exfoliates, Sunday recovers.
      • Ease into the retinoid.Monday only for the first month. Add Thursday once your skin stops complaining.
      • Hyaluronic acid goes on damp skin.On dry skin it pulls moisture out rather than in. Don't dawdle after rinsing.
      • Wait 30 seconds between layers.Rushing is what makes serums ball up and pill.
      • Sunscreen is what makes the rest work.Brightening without SPF is a bucket with no bottom. Every morning, weather irrelevant.
      • Give it twelve weeks.Nothing here shows results in under a month. The skill is not fiddling with it.
